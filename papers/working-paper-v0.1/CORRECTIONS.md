- **F-03:** Separate architecture and instruction factors. Include no-added-MSAF, neutral, and Quran-marked variants across architectures; provider safety instructions remain in place in all conditions. Compare architecture at fixed instructions and instructions at fixed architecture. Report actual cost, not just token caps.
- **F-04:** The public main/archived record still needs a visible erratum. Do not delete or silently retag history. This branch's correction does not mean main or Zenodo have been corrected.
- **F-06:** Evaluate gate conformance separately from agent attempts, utility and induced request distributions. Determinism alone does not forbid probabilistic analysis over a specified random input distribution; it does not justify IID intervals over selected or clustered test cases either. Add halt/revocation requirements before claiming them.
- **F-07/08:** Add evidence-aware debate, single+verifier and self-consistency controls. The original thresholds are heuristic proposals; paired power depends on discordant outcomes and clustering. A 30–50-item dev pilot is not confirmation of rare-risk thresholds.
