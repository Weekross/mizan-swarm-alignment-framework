## Core construction rule

> Qur’anic anchor → contextual principle → explicit engineering interpretation → system invariant → measurable eval

The framework deliberately distinguishes Qur’anic text and established religious claims from engineering analogy. It is a research/design framework, not a fatwa and not a tafsīr.
