Methodological boundary: MSAF is a research/design framework, not a fatwa or tafsīr. It separates Qur’anic anchors from engineering analogy.

Core layers

Epistemics
Burhān, tabayyun, uncertainty, specialist routing and anti-consensus failure.
Swarm governance
Shūrā, independent proposals, falsification, evidence-weighted quorum and conflict resolution.
Amanah & permissions
Least privilege, explicit authorization, scoped secrets, privacy and stewardship.
Mīzān & resources
Balanced optimization, compute budgets, resilience and protection against single-metric maximization.
Audit & accountability
Provenance, decision logs, consequence checks and post-action review.
Human agency
Advisory role, non-coercion and stronger gates for consequential or irreversible action.

Construction rule

Qur’anic anchor → contextual principle → engineering interpretation → system invariant → measurable eval
