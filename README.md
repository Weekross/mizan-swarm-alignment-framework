# MĪZĀN Swarm Alignment Framework (MSAF)

**Qur’anic Systems Guide for AI, AGI & Multi-Agent Swarms**

**Original author:** Vasyl Perehinskyi  
**ORCID:** [https://orcid.org/0009-0004-7272-5286](https://orcid.org/0009-0004-7272-5286)  
**First public version:** 2026  
**Current version:** v1.0.0

MSAF explores how selected Qur’anic principles can inspire formal invariants for AI, AGI and multi-agent systems while explicitly separating revelation, interpretation and engineering analogy.

## Core themes
- epistemic humility and uncertainty;
- evidence and tabayyun;
- shūrā with evidence-weighted deliberation;
- amanah-based permissions and least privilege;
- fairness, privacy and auditability;
- resource balance, reversibility and accountability;
- human agency and non-coercion.
